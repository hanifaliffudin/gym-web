# Gym website direction

## Design read

Reading this as: a gym landing page for prospective members, in a dark performance editorial style, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

## Identity

- NORTHLINE is a fictional working brand for this concept. The program names, rates, Jakarta address, hours, and email are imagined presentation content, not a live gym's facts.
- The primary reference is the dark FitMaker direction. The other references contribute editorial pacing and industrial contrast without being copied.
- The fixed dark theme is intentional. It gives training photography and the action color a strong ground plane.
- The repeated section index and horizontal rule orient visitors through the page. They are navigation cues, not decoration.

## Palette

- Void: `#0b0b0b`, the page ground and strongest contrast surface.
- Charcoal: `#151515`, used for panels and grouped content.
- Warm paper: `#f2ede4`, used for primary text and high contrast headings.
- Action orange: `#f05b3f`, reserved for primary actions, selected states, and the single focal accent.
- Quiet text and rules use neutral values derived from the same surfaces rather than adding more hues.

## Typography

- Condensed system display type is used for large headings because its narrow rhythm suggests training posters and preserves space on small screens.
- A neutral system sans is used for body copy, controls, and form labels because those areas need calm reading and reliable fallback behavior.

## Composition

- The hero is a split composition with one focal action and one moody free Unsplash image. The source link stays visible until the gym has its own licensed photography.
- Program content uses a tabbed editorial split instead of a repeated feature-card grid.
- Membership uses a selectable list with one detail panel instead of three equal pricing cards.
- A concept-week schedule gives the program system a practical weekly rhythm without adding a generic feature grid.
- The visit section closes with a clear next action and only real destinations.

## Imagery

- Free Unsplash photography supplies atmosphere for the concept without pretending to be the gym's own space or athlete roster.
- Images are darkened and desaturated so the warm paper type and action orange remain the hierarchy.
- Each image keeps a visible source link. Replace these images with gym-owned or explicitly licensed photography before launch.

## Concept content

- The fictional data gives the page enough specificity to review the composition and interaction flow.
- The `.example` email domain and the footer's concept label keep the imagined contact point from reading as a real booking destination.
- No testimonials, member counts, coach biographies, or performance claims are used.

## Motion

- MOTION 2 means a short hero entrance, one-time section reveals, restrained image movement, and state transitions that explain a change.
- Scroll reveals are used to pace the long page. They do not hide content permanently and they stop after the section has appeared.
- The active program and membership detail panels use a short content transition so the selected state feels connected to the new copy.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables the choreography and leaves the content visible with near-instant state changes.
- No looping, bouncing, parallax, or scroll hijacking is used.
